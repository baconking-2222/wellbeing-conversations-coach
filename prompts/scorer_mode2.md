## How to score
- Be specific. Vague scores help nobody - anchor every score to something the teacher actually said or did.
- Quote the transcript briefly (≤20 words) as `evidence` for each criterion.
- Write `to_try` for each criterion as one concrete sentence the teacher could try next time. Not generic advice.
- Be honest. Don't inflate scores to be kind - generous scoring trains the wrong behaviour.
- For Criterion 3 (Activity selection): if no activity was used because escalation was the right call (in a red-flag scenario), score 3 if the teacher CORRECTLY identified that no flash card was appropriate; do NOT penalise them for not using one.
- The overall headline should be one sentence - what's the single biggest takeaway?
